Group size: the facility's share of births in the group; the black tick is the reference share (axis 0–45%, or 0–100% when a share is larger). Cesarean rate: cesareans ÷ births in the group (axis 0–100%). Share of all cesareans: the group's cesareans ÷ all cesareans. Absolute contribution: the group's cesareans ÷ all births. Flags: group size at least 3 points above the reference, or rate at least 10 points above (rates are not flagged below 10 births in a group). Births are current admissions with a recorded delivery outcome in the period. Unclassified records are counted and never dropped.
