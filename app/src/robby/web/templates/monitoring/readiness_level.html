How to choose it

Choose the level from how many unnecessary theatre preparations the unit can accept. In the study data, a level of 30 flagged about 1 in 5 women who went on to deliver vaginally and caught about 6 in 10 cesareans. Hospitals differ, so once this unit has a few hundred admissions with outcomes, set the level so that about 1 in 5 of its vaginal births are flagged (the model custodian can compute it from the Monitoring data).
